Privacy policy

Chores for iPhone · Suomeksi · Home

Last updated 21 August 2026.

Chores is a household chore chart. This policy covers the iPhone app and the server it talks to. It is written to be read rather than skimmed past, so it says plainly what the app stores and what it does not.

Who is responsible

Ari Metsähalme is the controller of the data described here. Contact: arime-chores@proton.me.

What the app stores

Everything below is entered by the people using the app, and is stored so that a family sees the same chart on every one of their devices:

  • The family's name.
  • The display names of the parents and children in it. A first name or a nickname is all the app asks for, and all it needs.
  • The chores, the repeating weekly schedule, and the record of which chore was ticked off on which day.
  • For a parent who signs in with Apple: the anonymous identifier Apple issues for this app. It lets the same parent return to the same family after reinstalling. The app does not ask Apple for a name or an email address, so neither is received or stored.
  • For a child's device, and for a parent who joined with a code rather than an Apple ID: an anonymous session identifier created by the server. It is not tied to an Apple ID, an email address or a phone number.

What it does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting service. There is no analytics SDK in the app to collect anything behind its back.
  • No tracking, in Apple's sense or any other: nothing about you is linked with data from other apps or websites, and nothing is shared with a data broker.
  • No access to location, contacts, photos, the microphone or the camera. The app never asks, because it never uses them.
  • Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared for anyone else's marketing.

Children

The app is used by families, and a parent sets it up. Children do not create accounts, give an email address, or enter anything about themselves: a parent adds a child by first name, and hands the child's device a short code that connects it to the family. Everything stored about a child is what the parent typed, plus which chores the child ticked off. The app has no messaging, no social features, no in-app purchases, no external links inside the child's side of the app, and nothing that could show a child content from anyone else.

Where it is stored

Family data is held in a managed PostgreSQL database run by Supabase in the European Union, and is reached over HTTPS. Row-level security in the database means a device can read and write only the rows belonging to its own family. The database itself enforces that, rather than the app asking nicely. Supabase acts as a processor. A copy of the current chart is also cached on the device, so the app works with no signal.

Notifications

The daily reminder is scheduled by the app on the device itself. There are no push servers and no push tokens: nothing about a reminder leaves the phone. Declining the notification permission simply means no reminder.

How long it is kept, and how to delete it

Family data is kept until someone deletes it. The app can do all of it, with no request to anyone:

  • Manage → Delete account deletes the parent's sign-in for Chores and removes them from the family.
  • Manage → Leave this family gives up a place in the family.
  • When the last parent does either, the family, the children, the chores and the whole history go with it.
  • Swiping a child in Manage → People and tapping Delete deletes that child and their history.

These deletions are immediate and cannot be undone. Deleting the app removes the cached copy on the device. It does not delete the family from the server, since the other devices in the family are still using it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask for access to your data, its correction or its erasure, and for a copy of it. The app itself is the fastest route to all of these, and for erasure it is complete. For anything the app cannot do, write to arime-chores@proton.me. If you believe the data has been handled wrongly, you may complain to your national supervisory authority — in Finland, the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman.

The lawful basis for storing the data above is the performance of the service you asked for: without the family's names, chores and schedule there is no chore chart.

Changes

If what the app stores ever changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top moves. There is no mailing list to notify, because the app collects no address to notify one at.
