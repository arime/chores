Chores for iPhone · Suomeksi · Home

Chores is a chore chart for one household. A parent keeps the list of children and chores and sets a repeating weekly schedule. Each child sees their own chores for the day on their own device and ticks them off.

Setting up the first device

  1. Open the app and tap I'm a parent, then sign in with Apple. The sign-in is what lets you back into the same family after reinstalling the app or changing phone.
  2. Give the family a name and yourself a name. That is the whole of the setup.
  3. In Manage → People, add each child. In Manage → Chores, add the chores. In Manage → Schedule, pick a weekday and assign chores to children — that pattern then repeats every week.

Setting up a child's device

  1. On your own device, go to Manage → People, tap the child, and tap Show setup code.
  2. On the child's device, open the app and tap I have a code, then type the code in.

The device then belongs to that child, and stays that way — the child sees only their own chores, and nothing that lets them change the list. A code is good for seven days, and generating a new one cancels the old. A second parent joins the same way, with a code generated from their entry under People.

The reminder

A child's device offers a daily reminder at four in the afternoon on days that have chores. It is scheduled on the phone itself, so it works with no signal, and there is nothing to configure: allow notifications when the app asks and it appears. Declining is fine — the app just never notifies. Notifications can be turned back on later in the iPhone's Settings → Notifications → Chores.

"Can't reach the server"

The app shows this when it cannot get to its backend. In order of likelihood:

  • The device is offline. Chores keeps working from the copy on the device, and catches up on its own once the connection returns — anything ticked off in the meantime is not lost.
  • The backend is temporarily unavailable. Try again in a few minutes.

A child's device says its place is gone

That happens when the child's entry was removed on the parent's device, so the device no longer belongs to anyone. Add the child again under Manage → People and hand the device a new code.

Getting out, and deleting everything

Under Manage:

  • Sign out keeps your place in the family. Signing back in with Apple returns you to it.
  • Leave this family gives up your place. Getting back in needs a new code from the other parent.
  • Delete account deletes your Apple sign-in for Chores and removes you from the family.

If you are the only parent, leaving or deleting your account deletes the family, the children, the chores and all their history. That is immediate and cannot be undone. See the privacy policy for what is stored and where.

Still stuck

Write to arime-chores@proton.me. Say which iPhone and which iOS version, and what the screen said — it is usually enough to work out what happened.
