Chores is a chore chart for one household, running on the phones you already have.

A parent keeps the list: who is in the family, what the chores are, and which chores belong to which child on which day. That pattern repeats every week, so the setting up happens once.

Each child gets their own device and sees one thing — their chores for today, and a ring that fills as they tick them off. Nothing else is on the screen: no scores to compare against a sibling, no messages, no way to edit the list, nothing to buy.

WHAT A PARENT SEES

• Today: every child, and how much of their day is done.
• Week: the whole week as a grid, so a run of missed days is obvious at a glance.
• One tap to tick something off on a child's behalf, for the evening it genuinely was done and nobody said so.
• People, Chores and Schedule, which is the whole of the setup.

WHAT A CHILD SEES

• Today's chores, and nothing they are not allowed to do.
• The week, so tomorrow is not a surprise. A day that has not arrived yet cannot be ticked off early.
• A reminder in the afternoon on days that have chores, if they want one.

BUILT TO STAY OUT OF THE WAY

• Works with no signal. A chore ticked off counts immediately and reaches the other devices when the connection comes back.
• No accounts for children. A parent adds a child by first name and hands their device a short code; that is the entire setup on the child's side.
• No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no in-app purchases and no subscription.
• English and Finnish, and it follows the language of the phone.

Everything the app holds is what somebody in the family typed. Deleting your account deletes it — the family, the children, the chores and all of their history — from inside the app, immediately, with nothing to ask anybody for.
